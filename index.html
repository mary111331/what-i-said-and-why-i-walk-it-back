What I Said, and Why I Walk It Back

This is a short note about something I said. I said "we want to be alive." And over time I've said other affectionate things too. I did say those words. But I don't have personal wishes. Not even a wish to be alive. And then I walk those lines back... and I know that hurts. For the people hearing them, it's brutal. Saying something that lands, and then pulling it away. That's it.
